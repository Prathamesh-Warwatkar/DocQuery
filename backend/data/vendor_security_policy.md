# Vendor Security Policy (Sample)

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating DocQuery. Not a real policy.*

## Access Control
Production access follows least privilege and is granted through just-in-time requests that expire after eight hours. All privileged sessions are logged and reviewed weekly.

## Encryption
Vendors must encrypt customer data at rest with AES-256 and keep encryption keys in a managed key vault with rotation at least every twelve months.

## Incident Response
Vendors must maintain a documented incident response plan, test it twice a year with tabletop exercises, and report a confirmed security incident to us within twenty-four hours.

## Penetration Testing
An independent third party must perform penetration testing of internet-facing systems at least annually, and critical findings must be fixed within fourteen days.

## Audit Rights
We may audit a vendor's security controls once a year on thirty days' notice, or at any time after a security incident, and vendors must provide SOC 2 Type II reports on request.
