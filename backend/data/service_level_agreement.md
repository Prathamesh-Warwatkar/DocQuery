# Service Level Agreement (Sample)

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating DocQuery. Not a real contract.*

## Uptime Commitment
Provider commits to a monthly uptime percentage of 99.9 percent for the production platform, measured across each calendar month excluding scheduled maintenance.

## Support Response Times
Severity 1 incidents, where the platform is unavailable, receive a first response within thirty minutes around the clock. Severity 2 incidents receive a response within four business hours and Severity 3 requests within two business days.

## Service Credits
If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, Customer receives a credit of ten percent of the monthly fee, increasing to twenty-five percent if uptime is below 99 percent. Credits must be claimed within thirty days and are the sole remedy for downtime.

## Scheduled Maintenance
Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 01:00 and 05:00 UTC and is announced at least seven days ahead. Maintenance windows are capped at eight hours per month.

## Exclusions
Downtime caused by Customer's equipment, force majeure events, third-party outages outside Provider's control, or abuse of the platform does not count against the uptime commitment.
