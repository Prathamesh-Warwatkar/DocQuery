# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Sample)

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating DocQuery. Not a real contract.*

## Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information means any non-public business, technical or financial information disclosed by one party to the other, whether in writing, orally or by inspection of facilities. It excludes information that is publicly available, already known to the recipient, or independently developed without use of the disclosed material.

## Permitted Disclosure
The recipient may share Confidential Information only with employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound by written duties at least as strict as this NDA. Disclosure required by law or court order is allowed if the recipient gives prompt notice so the discloser can seek a protective order.

## Duration of Confidentiality
Obligations under this NDA last for five years after the date of the last disclosure. Trade secrets stay protected for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## Return or Destruction
Within ten business days of a written request, the recipient must return or securely destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing. Backup copies in routine archives may be kept but remain subject to confidentiality.

## Remedies
Unauthorized use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm, so the discloser may seek injunctive relief without posting a bond, in addition to other remedies available at law.
