# Remote Work Policy (Sample)

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating DocQuery. Not a real policy.*

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely after completing six months of service and with their manager's written approval. Roles that require on-site equipment or customer presence are not eligible.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor and covers a one-time home office stipend of 20,000 rupees. Equipment remains company property and must be returned within seven days of leaving the company.

## Working Hours
Remote employees keep a core overlap window from 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time for meetings and collaboration. Overtime requires prior manager approval.

## Information Security at Home
Work must be done on company-managed devices over the corporate VPN. Employees must lock their screens when away, never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN, and report a lost device within two hours.

## Expense Reimbursement
Internet costs up to 1,500 rupees per month are reimbursed on submission of a bill. Expense claims must be filed within thirty days of the purchase date.
