# Master Services Agreement (Sample)

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating DocQuery. Not a real contract.*

## Term and Renewal
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six months. After the initial term it renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety days before the current term ends.

## Fees and Payment
Customer shall pay all invoices within forty-five days of receipt. Late amounts accrue interest at one and a half percent per month. Fees for the subscription are fixed for the initial term and may increase by no more than five percent on each renewal.

## Termination for Cause
Either party may end this Agreement if the other commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty days after written notice. Provider may suspend access immediately if Customer's non-payment exceeds sixty days. On termination Customer must stop using the platform and pay all fees accrued.

## Limitation of Liability
Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages, including lost profits. The cap does not apply to breach of confidentiality or to indemnification for intellectual property infringement.

## Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Disputes shall first go to executive negotiation for fifteen days and then to binding arbitration in Wilmington under AAA rules.
