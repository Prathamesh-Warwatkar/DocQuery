# Data Processing Addendum (Sample)

*Synthetic sample document for demonstrating DocQuery. Not a real contract.*

## Roles of the Parties
Customer is the controller of Personal Data and Provider acts as processor. Provider will process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including with regard to transfers to third countries.

## Sub-processors
Customer gives general authorization for Provider to engage sub-processors. Provider shall publish an up-to-date list and notify Customer thirty days before adding a new sub-processor, and Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds.

## Security Measures
Provider maintains encryption of Personal Data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Access is restricted by role-based controls and multi-factor authentication, and is reviewed every quarter.

## Breach Notification
Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight hours after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach. The notice describes the nature of the breach, affected data categories, and the measures taken or proposed to mitigate it.

## Data Retention and Deletion
After the Agreement ends Provider will delete or return Personal Data within ninety days, at Customer's choice, and certify deletion on request. Encrypted backups are purged on their normal cycle, which does not exceed one hundred and eighty days.
